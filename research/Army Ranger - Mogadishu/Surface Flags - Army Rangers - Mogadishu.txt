/************************************************************************
**
**		Surface Attributes File
**
** This file contains the following surface related definitions:
**
**	Global
**  Surface
**
**
** NOTES:  
**
**  1) All definition properties (i.e., [Surface0]) ARE case sensitive.
**	   If you are adding a new definition, it is best to start with a 
**     copy of an existing (working) definition.
**	2) Name properties are NOT case sensitive (i.e., Name = "Joe" is the same
**	   as Name = "JOE")
**  3) All time is specified in seconds (unless otherwise noted)
**  4) The Float type is the same as Real (i.e., 1.0 or 1.5)
**  5) The valid range of all RGB color properties is between
**     0 and 255 (e.g., (0, 255, 0) would be green).
**
**
** 	TEXTURE ID SUMMARY:
** 
**  ----Internal ID's----
**	
**   0 = Unknown
**   1 = Air
**   2 = Flesh
**   3 = Armor (human)
** 110 = Sky
** 200 = Ladder
** 201 = Liquid
** 202 = Invisible
**
**  ----Custom ID's----
**
**   8 = Concrete (village outer walls, pillars, road)
**   9 = Plaster (soft interior walls)
**  10 = Metal 
**  11 = Wood
**  12 = Grass
**  13 = Dirt
**  14 = Tile (and other ceramics)
**  15 = Brick
**  16 = SpawnWater (Spawns leaking water particles)
**  17 = SpawnSand (Spawns leaking sand particles)
**  18 = Foliage (Plant debris)
**  19 = SpawnFlour (Spawns a cloud of flour after shooting things like food sacks)
**  20 = Glass
**  21 = Cloth
**  22 = Sand
**  23 = Snow
**  24 = Alpha Shootsies
**  25 = Player Blood
**  26 = No Shootsies
*/


/************************************************************************
**
** GLOBAL
**
** The global definition defines values that are global to all surfaces.
**
*/

/************************************************************************
**
** SURFACE
**
** The Surface definitions define all of the attributes associated with
** a particular surface.  These definitions *usually* map directly to 
** a particular texture flag (i.e., the Id property = a texture flag value).
** However, there are some pre-defined surfaces defined in this file that
** are needed by the game.  These surfaces are defined below and SHOULD
** NOT be removed from this file.  Also, it is important that the Ids used
** by these surfaces are NOT changed.  These pre-defined surfaces are:
**
**  UNKNOWN (id 0)		- An unknown surface is encountered
**  AIR	(id 1)			- Indicates a "non" surface
**  FLESH (id 2)		- Human/animal surfaces 
**  ARMOR (id 3)		- Human Armor surfaces 
**  SKY (id 110)		- Sky surface	  
**  LADDER (id 200)		- Ladder volume brush
**  LIQUID (id 201)		- Liquid volume brush
**  INVISIBLE (id 202)	- An invisible surface
**
**
** All surface definitions should contain the following properties:
**
**  Name (String)
**
**      The User friendly name of the surface (what shows up in DEdit).
**
**  Id (Integer)
**
**      This identifies the surface and usually maps directly to a texture
**      flag value.  This value must be between 0 and 255, and NOT be one
**      of the pre-defined values above (i.e., NOT 0, 1, 2, 3, 110, 200, 201 
**      or 202).
**
**  ShowsMark (Integer)
**
**		Do bullet holes and/or scortch marks show up on this surface.
**		(1 = true, 0 = false)
**
**  ShowBreath (Integer)
**
**		Show cold breath if a character is standing on this surface (i.e., 
**		the surface is ice/snow/outside/etc.)
**		(1 = true, 0 = false)
**
**  CanSeeThrough (Integer)
**
**		Can the player/ai see through this surface.
**		(1 = true, 0 = false)
**
**  CanShootThrough (Integer)
**
**		Can weapons shoot through this surface.  If set to true, the surface
**		can be shot through if the thickness of the object (or brush) is less 
**		than the MaxShootThroughThickness value defined below.
**		(1 = true, 0 = false)
**
**  MaxShootThroughPerturb (Integer)
**
**		Indicates that maximum peturb added to bullets when the go through
**		the surface (a random value between 0 and MaxShootThroughPerturb).
**
**  MaxShootThroughThickness (Integer)
**
**		The thickness of objects/brushes using this surface that bullets can 
**		no longer pass through them (e.g., if MaxShootThroughThickness = 31 
**		then bullets would not pass through a brush that is 32 units thick).
**		For objects/brushes that can always be shot through (e.g., liquid),
**		set MaxShootThroughThickness to 0.
**
**  BulletHoleSpr (String)
**
**		Name of the bullet hole sprite file.
**
**  BulletHoleMinScale (Float)
**
**		The minimum amount to scale the bullet hole sprite on this surface
**
**  BulletHoleMaxScale (Float)
**
**		The maximum amount to scale the bullet hole sprite on this surface
**
**  BulletRangeDampen (Float)
**
**		The amount the range of this bullet is damped when impacting this
**		surface (when CanShootThrough is 1).  This value is scaled based
**		on the thickness of the object/brush being shot through.  Valid values
**		are between 0.0 and 1.0 (e.g., 0.5 cuts the remaining range in half
**		if the brush is MaxShootThroughThickness.  A value of 1.0 doesn't
**		change the range).
**
**  BulletDamageDampen (Float)
**
**		The amount the damage of this bullet is damped when impacting this
**		surface (when CanShootThrough is 1).  This value is scaled based
**		on the thickness of the object/brush being shot through.  Valid values
**		are between 0.0 and 1.0 (e.g., 0.5 cuts the damage of the bullet in half
**		if the brush is MaxShootThroughThickness.  A value of 1.0 doesn't
**		change the damage).
**
**	BulletImpactSnd1-2 (String)
**
**		Name of the bullet impact sounds
**
**	ProjectileImpactSnd1-2 (String)
**
**		Name of the projectile impact sounds
**
**	MeleeImpactSnd1-2 (String)
**
**		Name of the melee impact sounds
**
**	ShellImpactSnd1 (String)
**
**		Specifies the shell impact (bounce) sound.
**
**	ShellSndRadius (Float)
**
**		Specifies the radius for the shell sound.
**
**	GrenadeImpactSnd1 (String)
**
**		Specifies the grenade impact (bounce) sound.
**
**	GrenadeSndRadius (Float)
**
**		Specifies the radius for the shell sound.
**
**	RtFootPrintSpr (String)
**
**		Name of the sprite used for right-footed footprints (use "" if
**		this surface doesn't show footprints)
**
**	LtFootPrintSpr (String)
**
**		Name of the sprite used for left-footed footprints (use "" if
**		this surface doesn't show footprints)
**
**  FootPrintLifetime (Float)
**
**		How long the footprint sprites stay around.
**
**	FootPrintScale (Vector)
**
**		Scale for the foot print sprites.
**
**	RtFootSnd1-2 (String)
**
**		Name of the 1st and 2nd right footstep sounds.
**
**	LtFootSnd1-2 (String)
**
**		Name of the 1st and 2nd left footstep sounds.
**
**	SnowmobileSnd1-2 (String)
**
**		Name of the 1st and 2nd snowmobile 1-off sounds.
**
**	BodyFallSnd (String)
**
**		Name of the sound a dead body makes when it lands on this surface.
**
**	BodyFallSndRadius (Float)
**
**		Radius for the BodyFallSnd sound.
**
**	BodyLedgeFallSnd (String)
**
**		Name of the sound a dead body makes when it lands on this surface after 
**      falling off a ledge.
**
**	BodyLedgeFallSndRadius (Float)
**
**		Radius for the BodyLedgeFallSnd sound.
**
**	ActivationSnd (String)
**
**		Name of the sound played when the player tries to activate this 
**		surface (i.e., the player uses the "use" key on the surface).  This
**		can be used to play a machine activate sound, or a "locked door" sound.
**
**	ActivationSndRadius (Float)
**
**		Radius for the ActivationSnd sound.
**
**	DeathNoiseMod (Float)
**
**		This is a modifier used to increase/decrease how loud the death
**		noise (body fall sound) is for this surface by scaling the radius.  
**		This used by the AI to determine if they could hear the sound or not. 
**
**	MoveNoiseMod (Float)
**
**		This is a modifier used to increase/decrease how loud movement
**		sounds are on this surface by scaling the radius.  This used by 
**		the AI to determine if they could hear the sound or not. 
**
**	ImpactNoiseMod (Float)
**
**		This is a modifier used to increase/decrease how loud impact
**		sounds are on this surface by scaling the radius.  This used 
**		by the AI to determine if they could hear the sound or not.
**
**	Hardness (Float)
**
**		This specifies the hardness of the surface.  It is currently used
**		to determine how much grenades bounce off the surface.  Valid values
**		are between 0.0 (SOFT - grenades stick to surface), and 1.0 (HARD -
**		grenades don't lose any velocity when the bounce off the surface).
**
**	SnowmobileVelMult (Float)
**
**		This specifies a velocity multiplier for the snowmobile when on this
**		surface.  This is used to slow down the snowmobile on certain surfaces.
**		Valid values are between 0.0 (stopped) and 1.0 (normal speed).
**
**	Magnetic (Integer)
**
**		This specifies if the surface is magnetic (Actually, if the surface
**		attracts magnetic objects - i.e., is metal).  (1 = true, 0 = false)
**
**	ImpactFXName (string) (OPTIONAL)
**
**		Specifies the optional name of a FxED created FX to use when a weapon
**		impacts this surface.  For example:
**
**			ImpactFXName = "StoneImpactFX01"
**
**	UWImpactFXName	(string) (OPTIONAL)
**
**		Specifies the optional name of a FxED created FX to use when a weapon 
**		impacts this surface under water.  For example:
**
**			UWImpactFXName = "UWMetalImpactFX01"
**
**	ExitFXName (string) (OPTIONAL)
**
**		Specifies the optional name of a FxED created FX to use when a weapon
**		exits this surface.  For example:
**
**			ExitFXName = "WoodExitFX01"
**
**	UWExitFXName (string) (OPTIONAL)
**
**		Specifies the optional name of a FxED created FX to use when a weapon
**		exits this surface under water.  For example:
**
**			UWExitFXName = "UWClothExitFX01"
**
**	SnowmobileImpactFXName (String) (OPTIONAL)
**
**		Specifies the optional name of a FxED created FX to use when a snowmobile
**		collides with this surface.  For example:
**
**			SnowmobileImpactFXName = "SnowmobileImpact_Snowy"	
**		
*/
